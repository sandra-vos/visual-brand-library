# Pro-Vital Infographic Style

## Purpose

This Reference defines the Pro-Vital-specific visual expression of infographics.

All infographic information architecture, semantic pattern selection, hierarchy, data integrity, final-size readability and general infographic QA inherit from `framework/visual/infographic-standard.md`.

This Reference MUST NOT duplicate or weaken those general rules.

## Relationship to Illustration Style

After the Framework Infographic Standard has resolved the semantic structure, Pro-Vital infographic elements MUST follow `illustration-style.md`.

That includes the approved:

- illustration palette;
- solid and controlled-gradient behaviour;
- shape language;
- light 2.5D depth;
- small-icon readability;
- botanical and scientific vocabulary.

Information clarity takes precedence over adding a familiar Brand motif.

## Pro-Vital Expression

Pro-Vital infographics SHOULD feel:

- clear and approachable;
- fresh and contemporary;
- organic without becoming decorative;
- informative without becoming clinical;
- visually coherent with other Pro-Vital illustrations.

The composition MAY combine organic and geometric forms where the semantic structure benefits from them.

## Botanical and Scientific Balance

Botanical elements MAY support concepts such as natural origin, ingredients, growth or vitality.

Scientific and geometric elements MAY support concepts such as composition, research, nutrients, quantities or functional relationships.

Neither vocabulary is mandatory.

Do not attach a leaf, flower, molecule, hexagon or other Brand-associated element to every infographic simply to make it look Pro-Vital.

## Visual Richness

Small infographic icons SHOULD remain restrained and immediately recognisable.

Larger infographic compositions MAY create richness through:

- overlap;
- grouping;
- scale variation;
- controlled combinations of simple illustrated elements;
- the approved Pro-Vital colour palette.

Richness SHOULD come from composition rather than unnecessary internal detail.

## Brand-Specific Rejection Criteria

In addition to the Framework Infographic Standard, reject or revise a Pro-Vital infographic when:

- Brand decoration reduces semantic clarity;
- botanical elements are added without communicative or compositional purpose;
- scientific symbols imply unsupported meaning;
- the illustration language conflicts with `illustration-style.md`;
- the composition becomes excessively clinical, technical, childish or decorative;
- colour, gradient or depth treatment drifts from the approved Pro-Vital illustration family.

## Reference Priority

For Pro-Vital infographics, apply:

1. `framework/visual/infographic-standard.md` for information design;
2. this Reference for Pro-Vital infographic expression;
3. `illustration-style.md` for illustrated building blocks;
4. `visual-style.md` for the broader Pro-Vital visual language;
5. Brand Guidelines and applicable Content/compliance requirements.

## Guiding Principle

> Use Pro-Vital styling to make well-structured information recognisably Pro-Vital; never use styling as a substitute for information design.
